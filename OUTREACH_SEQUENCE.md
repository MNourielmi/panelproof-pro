# First 20 prospects: disciplined validation

Target: 20 *qualified* businesses (panel builders, automation integrators, OEM machine builders). Find named organizations using their official sites, verify they work with FAT/SAT or panel documentation, and log a public business contact route. Avoid guessing email addresses. No outreach has been sent by this package.

## Initial message
Subject: Quick question about FAT/SAT paperwork

Hi,

I'm testing a small offline tool for control-panel FAT/SAT documentation. It records checks and exceptions and exports a printable report and CSV issue list.

I'm looking for feedback from panel builders rather than asking you to switch systems. Would someone on your team be open to a 10-minute demo and telling me what would make it useful?

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Your real contact details]

## Follow-up (only if appropriate, after 4–6 business days)
Hi, following up once on my FAT/SAT documentation question. I can share a short demo, or I would appreciate knowing whether this is a problem your team actually has. No worries if not relevant.

## Qualification questions
1. How many FAT/SAT reports do you create in a typical month?
2. Which step consumes the most time: writing checks, recording evidence, tracking exceptions, or formatting the final report?
3. What system do you use now, and what is frustrating about it?
4. Can your team use a local offline tool, or do you require approved enterprise software?
5. Would a CAD $49 evaluation be worth trying if it solves one recurring documentation problem? Why or why not?

## Go/no-go rule
After 20 qualified contacts, review actual responses. Continue only if at least 3 substantive discovery conversations occur and at least 1 prospect requests a pilot or clearly states a specific unmet need. Otherwise revise the positioning or consider a different problem; do not manufacture traction.
