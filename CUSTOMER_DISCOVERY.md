# PanelProof — customer discovery and first-sale playbook

## Target
Owner, commissioning manager, or lead technician at a small industrial control-panel shop or systems integrator.

## Offer
A five-minute demonstration of an offline FAT/SAT documentation prototype. Pilot price hypothesis: CAD 49, subject to agreed terms. Do not collect payments until licensing, refund terms, seller details, and delivery are ready.

## Demo sequence (5 minutes)
1. Open public_site/app.html; load sample project.
2. Show failed and blocked checks and missing evidence.
3. Add a custom check, change its status, and record a note.
4. Export issue CSV and JSON backup.
5. Print a report to PDF. Explain that the tool records human testing, not electrical measurements or certification.

## Discovery questions
- How do you currently create and update FAT/SAT checklists?
- How many hours does documentation take per panel?
- What must appear in your handover package?
- Would this fit your current workflow, and what is missing?
- Would you pay CAD 49 for a one-project pilot if the gaps were addressed?

## Outreach email
Subject: Quick FAT/SAT documentation demo for your panel shop

## Evidence and go/no-go
Record each contact and response in leads.csv. Do not invent contacts, endorsements, or savings. After 20 relevant outreaches, continue only if buyers describe a concrete problem and at least one requests a paid pilot or signs an agreement.
