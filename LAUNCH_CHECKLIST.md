# PanelProof Pro — Launch checklist

## Completed
- Offline application and sample data
- Printable report, issue and full-register CSV, JSON import/export
- Documentation-readiness warnings
- Public-facing static landing page (`landing.html`)
- Automated browser regression test (`browser_regression.py`)

## Before accepting money
- Test actual customer workflows and save/restore on Windows Edge and Chrome
- Confirm customer pain and desired report formats with at least 5 qualified buyers
- Establish seller identity, contact email, privacy and support policy
- Draft and review license, refund and liability terms appropriate to jurisdiction
- Set up seller-controlled checkout and delivery; do not embed unverified payment links
- Confirm tax obligations and whether the product meets buyer security/procurement requirements

## Suggested first customer experiment
1. Identify 20 real small panel shops or system integrators.
2. Send a short, truthful invitation for a 5-minute demo; do not claim certification or existing customers.
3. Show the sample project and a report; ask what their current process costs in time.
4. Ask whether they would pay CAD 49 for a pilot, and record objections.
5. Treat a completed payment or signed pilot agreement, not interest alone, as validation.

## Publish
Upload `landing.html` and `index.html` to a static host in the same directory. Review the public URL and mobile layout. The landing page is a draft until seller contact and legal terms are supplied.
