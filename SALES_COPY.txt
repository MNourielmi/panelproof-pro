PRODUCT: PanelProof — Offline FAT/SAT Documentation Builder

HEADLINE: Stop rebuilding electrical commissioning checklists from scratch.

DESCRIPTION: PanelProof is a lightweight, offline-first commissioning record builder for control panels, automation contractors, panel shops, and maintenance teams.
