# PanelProof Pro — Founding Customer Evaluation

**For:** electrical control-panel shops, machine builders, and automation integrators that repeatedly prepare FAT/SAT documentation.

**Problem:** checklists, punch lists, evidence notes, and handover paperwork are often spread across documents and spreadsheets.

**Offer:** an offline browser-based FAT/SAT documentation prototype that consolidates check statuses, evidence notes, exceptions, and exports in one place.

**What the buyer receives:** offline HTML application; 20 starting checks; ability to add project-specific checks; Pass/Fail/Blocked/N/A/Not Tested states; CSV issue and register exports; JSON backup; printable report; basic handover-readiness warnings.

**Proposed founding evaluation price:** CAD $49, one-time, only after the buyer sees a working demonstration and accepts written terms. **No payments are being accepted yet.**

**Pilot success criteria (to agree with buyer):** complete one non-sensitive sample FAT/SAT record; export an issue list; export a JSON backup; print a report; identify at least one workflow improvement worth keeping.

**Not included:** actual electrical testing, certified compliance checks, secure multi-user collaboration, cloud synchronization, guaranteed time savings, or regulated sign-off. Data is stored in the local browser and can be cleared; export backups. Do not enter confidential customer data in a public demo.

**Suggested sales question:** “Would you spend 10 minutes showing me how you currently assemble a FAT report? I can show you an offline alternative and you can tell me what it is missing.”

**Launch dependencies:** seller contact details, license/refund terms, payment mechanism, hosted demo, and real customer validation. None should be represented as complete before verified.
